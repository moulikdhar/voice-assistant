information :

Voxtral Realtime : the ears. Turns the caller's voice into text, live, while they speak.
Mistral Large : the brain. Reads what the caller said and decides what the AI says next. After the call it also writes the summary.
Voxtral TTS : the mouth. Turns the AI's reply into a natural human voice.

 LiveKit (the plumbing, open source) 
 LiveKit server  (`livekit-server.exe`): the phone line. Carries live audio between the caller's browser and the AI.
 LiveKit Agents : the call manager. Decides when the caller has finished talking, when the AI may speak, and stops the AI when the caller interrupts (barge-in).
 livekit-plugins-mistralai : the connector. Plugs the three Mistral models into LiveKit Agents.
 livekit-client  (browser): connects the phone screen to the phone line: sends your mic, plays the AI's voice.

 Silero VAD (small free model) 
 The speech detector. Tells LiveKit "a human is speaking right now" or "silence", which drives turn-taking and barge-in.
